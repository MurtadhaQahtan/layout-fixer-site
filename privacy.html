Privacy Policy – Layout Fixer (Arabic ⇄ English)

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Layout Fixer does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • Offline only. The app never connects to the internet. It has no analytics, telemetry, ads, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • Text you convert. When you press the hotkey, the app briefly reads the selected text (through a simulated copy) or the clipboard, converts it on your device, pastes it back, and then restores your previous clipboard text. The text stays in memory only and is never saved or sent.
  • Settings are stored in a local file on your PC and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft Store.
